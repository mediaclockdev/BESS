Why storage sits on campus

Grid support and backup, from one asset.

Modern battery energy storage systems are built for grid-scale applications, renewable integration, peak shaving, frequency regulation, arbitrage and backup power — including for data centres.

We target high efficiency, safety, scalability, long cycle life and genuine integration with renewables and smart grids. Sizing is adjusted to site conditions — cooling, grid connection through the local distribution network — and to what the customer actually needs.

System components

What's inside a typical installation.

Cells / modules / racks

The storage core

Lithium iron phosphate dominates on the balance of safety, cost, energy density around 120 Wh/kg, power density and cycle life. LFP is preferred over NMC for lower thermal runaway risk and more abundant materials.

BMS

Battery management system

Monitors voltage, current, temperature, state of charge and state of health at cell, module and rack level. Handles balancing, overcharge and short-circuit protection, and optimises performance and lifespan.

PCS

Power conversion system

Converts DC from the batteries to AC for the grid and back. Grid-forming inverters add synthetic inertia, voltage and frequency support, and black-start capability.

EMS

Energy management system

Oversees operation, optimisation, dispatch scheduling, market participation and integration with renewables or site load — using AI and machine learning for predictive maintenance.

TMS

Thermal management

HVAC, liquid cooling or air systems holding the pack within its optimal temperature range, preventing degradation and protecting safety across a variable climate.

Enclosures

Safety and containment

Containerised, weatherproof, seismic-rated outdoor enclosures with fire detection and suppression, gas detection and ventilation — complying with UL 9540 and NFPA 855.

Key design features

What we optimise for.

Safety first
LFP chemistry, advanced multi-level BMS monitoring, thermal runaway mitigation through isolation and suppression, and rigorous testing. Modern systems carry substantially lower fire risk than earlier designs.
Scalability
Containerised or modular racks for straightforward deployment and augmentation over time — scaling from megawatts to hundreds of megawatts, front-of-meter or behind-the-meter.
Efficiency
Round-trip efficiency commonly 85–95% or better, response under 150–200 milliseconds, and durations of one to four hours, pushing toward eight or more with newer approaches.
Intelligence
Hybrid-ready alongside solar and wind, grid-forming capability, reactive power support, and digital controls that stack multiple revenue streams.
Longevity
Robust enclosures, minimal maintenance, high cycle life, low self-discharge and environmental resilience, with designs that allow augmentation to counter degradation.
Footprint
High energy density for a compact site, with emphasis on recyclability, reduced use of critical materials, and alignment with net-zero commitments.
